# Day-of-Shoot Brief

**Category:** Production
**Owner:** [Producer / 1st AD if applicable]
**Last reviewed:** REPLACE

## Purpose

Crew arrives knowing the schedule, their role, the client expectation,
and the wrap target — so the shoot runs to time and everyone gets paid
right.

## When to use

Shoot day. Brief is delivered at call time, on-site, before any gear
goes out of the cases.

## Who's involved

- **Owns:** Producer (or DP if no producer on-site)
- **Supports:** All crew
- **Informs:** Client liaison

## Prerequisites

- Call sheet has been sent to crew at least 24h before
- Gear is on-site
- Producer has reviewed the run-of-show

## Step-by-step

1. **Crew arrival check** — everyone on the call sheet present at the
   call time. Any missing, call them now.
2. **Run the brief** (5 minutes, standing huddle):
   - What we're shooting and for whom
   - The deliverables (what the client is paying for)
   - The schedule with hard time gates (e.g. "we lose stage at 22:00")
   - Each crew member's role and primary responsibility
   - Where food and breaks are
   - Wrap target
3. **Confirm point-of-contact** with the client / venue. Get their
   phone number into the producer's phone.
4. **Walkthrough** the shoot location with the camera team — agree
   on positions, sight lines, and audio placement.
5. **Audio check** before any talent on stage. Run a level check from
   the board to the recorder.
6. **First card change** — every camera op confirms they know where
   their backup card is and when to swap.
7. **Comms check** — confirm everyone has the same channel / signal
   for emergency stop.
8. **Shoot.** Producer keeps the run-of-show open and notes anything
   that drifts from plan (these become post-mortem notes after wrap).

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Don't skip the standing brief because "everyone knows." Even with
  the same crew, the run-of-show changes per Job.
- Don't let camera ops set up before the audio team has identified
  cable runs — saves running over each other.
- Don't promise the client deliverables that aren't on the contract.
