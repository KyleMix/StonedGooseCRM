# First-Cut Review

**Category:** Post-production
**Owner:** [Editor]
**Last reviewed:** REPLACE

## Purpose

Get the first watchable cut in front of internal eyes (and then the
client) with structured feedback — so we don't churn through 6
revision rounds when 2 will do.

## When to use

First cut is ready for review. Internal review before client review.

## Who's involved

- **Owns:** Editor
- **Supports:** Producer (internal review), Client (external review)

## Prerequisites

- A self-contained playable export (Frame.io / Vimeo private / WeTransfer)
- Project structure follows [`post-shoot-data-wrangling.md`](./post-shoot-data-wrangling.md)

## Step-by-step

### Internal review

1. **Export a review version** (1080p, watermarked with Job code and
   "INTERNAL — not for distribution") and upload to the review tool.
2. **Send the link to the producer** with three questions:
   - Does this hit the deliverables in the contract?
   - Is there anything missing the client will ask for?
   - Any technical issues (audio drift, color jump, missing lower-third)?
3. **Wait for producer sign-off** before sending to client. Apply any
   notes.

### Client review

4. **Export a client review version** — same as above, with a
   watermark that says "FOR REVIEW — not final" and the Job code.
5. **Send via the review tool** with a short note:
   - Subject: `<Job name> — first cut for review`
   - Body: "Here's the first cut. Please leave timestamped notes by
     [REPLACE — deadline, typically +3 business days]."
6. **Set a Twenty task** for "Client feedback on first cut" with the
   deadline.
7. **Consolidate feedback** when received. If feedback contradicts the
   contract scope, flag it to the producer before applying.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- **Don't send the client an unwatermarked export.** They will share
  it, and someone will mistake it for final.
- Don't apply contradictory notes without raising — clients sometimes
  forget what they signed off on.
- Don't go past one round of revisions unless that's contracted.
