## 3. Independent contractor

Crew is engaged as an independent contractor, not as an employee.
Crew is responsible for own taxes, insurance, and benefits. This
agreement does not establish employment, partnership, or agency.

## 4. Ownership

All footage, audio, edits, and files created by Crew during this
engagement are work-for-hire owned exclusively by Producer. Crew
assigns all rights, including the right to license to Producer's
clients, to Producer.

## 5. Use in portfolio

Crew may include reference to the engagement (Producer name, job
type, role) in Crew's resume and portfolio. Crew may **not** publish
footage or stills from the engagement without Producer's written
consent.

## 6. Confidentiality

Crew agrees to treat client identities and unreleased event details
as confidential through the public release date of the deliverables.

## 7. Cancellation

- Producer-initiated cancellation more than 7 days before call time:
  no fee owed.
- 3–7 days before: 50% of day rate / project rate owed.
- Less than 3 days: full fee owed.
